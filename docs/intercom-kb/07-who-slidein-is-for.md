# Who SlideIn is for

SlideIn suits businesses whose buyers are found by research rather than by ads. What changes between industries is the audience and the angle. The workflow is the same.

Industries with a use-case page on our site:
US home lenders, mortgage brokers, mid-sized companies, insurance agencies, real estate firms, e-commerce brands, tech startups, corporate agencies, commercial banks, national banks, MFS providers, consumer lenders, telecom companies, large insurers, private hospitals, superstore chains, diagnostic centers, export houses, restaurant chains and local conglomerates.

See them at https://www.tanim.social/use-cases

## Rules that hold for every industry
- Runs inside your own server. Your data stays with you.
- Writes only to people who agreed to hear from you, with a one-click opt-out.
- You approve each campaign. Every email is checked before it goes out.
- Sent slowly from your own domain, and it pauses itself if complaints rise.
- Replies go straight to the right person on your team, the same day.
