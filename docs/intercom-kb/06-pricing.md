# Pricing

We manage your outreach emails every month, at a price that follows your volume.

- **500 outreach emails a month: $750 a month** ($1.50 per email). $750 is the monthly minimum.
- Bigger volumes get graduated discounts. Each band's rate applies only to the emails inside that band, so a bigger volume never costs less than a smaller one:

| Emails in a month | Price per email in this band |
|---|---|
| Up to 10,000 | $1.50 |
| 10,001 to 50,000 | about $0.75 |
| 50,001 to 200,000 | about $0.41 |
| Above 200,000 | about $0.20 |

The pricing page has a calculator for your exact monthly volume: https://www.tanim.social/pricing

Both ways of working (managed service or run it yourself) start with a walkthrough. For a quote outside these numbers, contact us.
