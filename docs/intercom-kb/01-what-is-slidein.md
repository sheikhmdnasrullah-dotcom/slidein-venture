# What is SlideIn?

SlideIn is an agentic system that finds and researches each prospect, matches them to your offers, and writes each one a personal email. It keeps working until they are convinced.

Most teams assemble outreach from disconnected tools: research in a spreadsheet, contact details collected by hand, emails written from nothing, sending on its own track, and silence after the send. SlideIn runs the whole loop in one workspace, so the research behind an email is never further away than the email itself.

Everything starts with your Master Business Playbook: your business details, offers and ideal client profile in one place. Every later stage reads from it, and it grows every time you use it.

## Two ways to work with it

- **Run it yourself:** you operate the workflow inside the platform.
- **We run it for you (managed service):** our team configures and operates the workflow for you.

Both use the same controls, the same approval step and the same records. See "Managed service or run it yourself" for details.
