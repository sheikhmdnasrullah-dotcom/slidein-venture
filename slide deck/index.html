The Three Bottlenecks in Mortgage Client Acquisition

Structural gaps that prevent consistent, predictable loan origination across branches.

01
The 6–12 Month Invisible Window
  • -Homebuyers research rates, affordability, and loan options for 6 to 12 months before contacting an advisor.
  • -Finaya currently has zero digital capture points during this high-intent research window.
02
Pure Referral Dependency
  • -Relying exclusively on realtor referrals creates fluctuating pipelines and seasonal dry spells.
  • -There is no automated, owned marketing engine pulling in new homebuyer leads consistently.
03
Local Search Absence
  • -When prospective buyers search for local mortgage advisors, Finaya EMAs do not rank prominently.
  • -Post-rebrand digital footprints require complete Google Business and social restructuring.
Core Insight: Every day without a digital acquisition infrastructure is a day local competitors capture these high-value loan transactions.
