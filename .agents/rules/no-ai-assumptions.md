# All-Time Rule: Content Integrity & Zero AI Assumptions

1. **Zero AI Assumption Text**: NEVER invent, assume, or hallucinate placeholder copywriting, fake metrics, fake case studies, fake clients, fake claims, or synthetic blog articles without explicit command from the user.
2. **Clean Design Intact**: Always preserve the clean, elevated aesthetic and design system tokens (typography scales, tone contracts, subtle paper textures, precise grid alignments, and component architecture) across all screens and breakpoints.
3. **User-Authored Content Truth**: In content modules (such as `content/insights.ts`, `content/framework.ts`, `content/contact.ts`), only render verified user-provided copy and data structures. When no user content exists, render pristine, minimal structural frames and clean states without fake filler.
