# What we can and cannot promise

Email delivery is measurable up to a point. Past that point nobody can promise anything.

- **Accepted is not delivered.** A receiving server accepting a message means the door opened. Delivered means the mailbox provider took it. Inbox placement is decided by the recipient's provider, and no sender controls it.
- **Suppression is permanent.** A "no" is suppressed across every sending domain. Nobody who asked to be left alone is contacted again.
- **A person is in the loop.** Every email is read before it sends and every reply is read by a person. Interested leads reach you the same day, with the research record attached.
- **Sending is configured, not improvised.** Dedicated domains, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are in place before the first send.
- **No open tracking.** The metric is not part of the platform.

SlideIn does not promise specific results such as reply rates or booked meetings.
