SlideIn · Brand guidelines01

One symbol. One wordmark.

The SlideIn identity is a symbol and a wordmark that were drawn to belong together. The symbol is two folded paper ribbons that slide over one another into an S. Where one sheet lies over the other they share exactly one square: the stop, the brand’s full stop in solid form. The wordmark is SlideIn. set in Fraunces, with the same orange full stop.

The favicon is the symbol’s smallest size, not a separate mark. Everything in this guide inherits from brand/source/slidein-symbol-master.svg.

SlideIn · Brand guidelines02

The symbol

Construction

Two ribbons, each 96 units wide on a 512 grid, turn 180° about the centre. Every corner is concentric (outer radius 104, inner 8). The shared square is 1 x, and x is the unit that measures the whole system: it is 24.5% of the symbol’s height.

Two optical sizes, one symbol

From 64 px up, the master geometry. At 48 px and below (the browser tab), the same construction drawn on a 16-pixel grid with heavier ribbons and tighter gaps, so edges land on whole pixels. They are not two designs.

Reading

Orange is dominant so the symbol survives both light and dark surfaces. The black stop appears only on light grounds; on black and dark grey it turns ivory (the reversed logo), because a black stop would disappear.

Dimensional version

The Blender render (brand/favicon/renders/master-3d.png) is a presentation of the same geometry, used for the large app icons. It never replaces the vector master.

SlideIn · Brand guidelines06

Wordmark

SlideIn. is set in Fraunces (SIL Open Font License 1.1), weight 600. Primary and stacked use optical size 48; the compact logo uses optical size 24 with slightly looser tracking, because fine hairlines fail at small sizes.

Tracking −0.02 em (−0.01 em compact). Kerning is the font’s own, plus one custom adjustment: the full stop is drawn 0.014 em closer to the n. Capital S and I are part of the name; the full stop is part of the mark and is always orange.

The wordmark ships as outlined paths, so no font is required. Source: brand/source/wordmark/ (extractor script, glyph data, licence).
